Hatzilu - Privacy Policy

Effective Date: Nov 5, 2026
Last Updated: Nov 10, 2026

Hatzilu is a personal safety SOS application developed by PriPriX. This policy explains exactly what data we collect, why we collect it, and with whom we share it. This policy matches our Google Play Data Safety declaration.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the data needed for SOS, account, and app improvement. We do NOT collect profile photos, videos, audio recordings, SMS inbox messages, health data, or browsing history.

a) Personal Info

  • Name: You provide in sign-up and profile. Used for account and SOS alert.
  • Email address: You provide for login and profile. Used for account login and included in WhatsApp SOS alert to your emergency contacts.
  • User IDs: Firebase UID created when you sign up. Used to identify your account. Not shared in SOS alert.
  • Address: Home address you provide in your profile. Used for account and included in WhatsApp SOS alert to your emergency contacts.
  • Phone number: Your phone number you provide. Used for account and included in SOS alert so contacts can call you back.

b) Precise Location

Location Permission must be enabled for Hatzilu to work. When you press SOS, we access your current precise GPS location to send it to your emergency contacts via SMS / WhatsApp. Location is used only for SOS. We do not collect your location history continuously and we do not track location when the app is closed.

c) Contacts

Emergency contacts you add in the app - name and phone number. These contacts are the recipients of your SOS alerts. We access your contacts only when you tap to add an emergency contact. We do not copy your full phone contact list.

d) Financial Info - Purchase History

We check your subscription status via Google Play Billing - monthly (Rs.30) and yearly (Rs.300). We receive only purchase history / subscription status to unlock premium. We do NOT receive or store your credit card number, bank details, or UPI PIN. Payment is processed securely by Google Play.

e) App Info and Performance

  • Crash logs: If app crashes, technical crash report is sent to Firebase Crashlytics to fix bugs.
  • Diagnostics: Technical diagnostics like device model, Android version, app version to fix performance issues.

Crash logs and Diagnostics are optional for app function. SOS works even without them. They are used only to improve app stability.

f) App Activity

App interactions: Which screens you open, SOS button press, taps. Collected via Firebase Analytics to understand which features are used and improve app. This is optional, SOS works even without it. This is not used to track you personally or for advertising.

g) Device or Other IDs

Device ID / Firebase ID. Used for push notifications, subscription verification, and analytics. Not used for advertising.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • App functionality: SOS alert, account login, subscription unlock.
  • Account management: Create account, sign-in, manage profile and subscription.
  • Analytics: Crash logs, Diagnostics, App interactions, Device IDs to improve app stability.

3. Data Shared

We do NOT sell your data to anyone. We do NOT share data for advertising.

  • With your Emergency Contacts via WhatsApp / SMS - SOS Alert Only: Name, Email address, Address, Phone number, Precise location. This happens only when you press SOS.
  • With Firebase / Google - Service Provider: Your account data (Name, Email address, Address, Phone number, User IDs) is stored in Firebase for account and authentication. Crash logs, Diagnostics, App interactions, Device IDs are also sent to Firebase for analytics and crash fixing. Firebase processes data on our behalf.
  • With Google Play Billing: Purchase history to verify subscription.
  • Not Shared in SOS Alert: User IDs and Purchase history are NOT shared in SOS alert to emergency contacts.

When you send SOS via WhatsApp, WhatsApp service also processes the message as per its own policy.

4. Data Storage and Security

Data is stored securely in Firebase with encryption and Security Rules. All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. We do not sell your data to third parties.

5. Permissions Explained

  • Location: Required. You must enable Location Permission for Hatzilu to work, to send SOS location to your emergency contacts.
  • Contacts: To add emergency contacts from your phone only when you choose to. If you type contacts manually, this permission is not used.
  • Internet: To sync account, SOS alerts and subscription status.
  • Billing: To process subscriptions via Google Play Secure Payment.

6. Subscriptions and Billing

Hatzilu offers monthly (Rs.30) and yearly (Rs.300 / Save 2 months) via Google Play. Free trial 2 days, one-time per device / email, server-enforced. All payments are secured by Google Play. Subscriptions auto-renew unless cancelled. Manage or cancel anytime via Google Play Store > Payments and Subscriptions.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your account data while your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data by emailing priprixtrader@gmail.com or apphatzilu@gmail.com or via this page. We will delete your data within 7 working days. You can also edit or delete emergency contacts and address from inside the app profile at any time.

8. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 18. It is for adults 18+. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
